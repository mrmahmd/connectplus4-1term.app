# Connect Plus Primary 4 - Term 1 (2026-2027)

## Source decision

- The Ministry Student's Book is the curriculum authority.
- El-Moasser is a supporting source for explanation depth, language notes, and question patterns.
- The old Ministry contents page still lists six units, but the actual book pages, Scope and Sequence, and the updated El-Moasser guide consistently contain five units. The rebuilt app therefore uses five units.
- El-Moasser groups Lessons 1-2, 3-4, and 5-6. The app separates every lesson into its own learning journey.

## Required lesson stations

Every lesson will contain:

1. Mission Overview
2. Vocabulary Flashcards with a simple English definition, contextual example, and image
3. Important Verbs
4. Language Notes
5. Reading Explorer with explanation points, not only a summary
6. Grammar Lab when the lesson contains grammar
7. Practice Arena with 30 contextual questions

Every unit ends with a 50-question mastery bank. Units unlock sequentially. Progress, exact question position, XP, coins, stars, and achievements remain connected to the portal cloud account.
